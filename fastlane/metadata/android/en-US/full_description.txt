Murmely is a free (as in freedom) Android photo and video editor.

Photos
• Filters: colour looks, geometric effects (swirl, mirror, kaleidoscope, ripple, glass tiles …) and your own shaders, with strength and a centre you can move
• Sliders: brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth and more
• Crop and turn, make the canvas larger (e.g. for a caption), frames inside or around the photo
• Draw, add texts (seven fonts, outline, letter spacing, shadows) and stickers; move, resize and turn them with your fingers
• Templates: save a look (sliders, filters, frame) and apply it to other photos with one tap
• Collages

Videos
• Crop, change speed, mute audio, stabilize, cut the start or end

• Dark and light themes
• 100% free and open source software, no proprietary dependencies, no ads, no tracking
