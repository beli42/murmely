Murmely is a free (as in freedom) photo and video editor for Android.

<b>Photos</b>
• Sliders: brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, sharpness, vignette, grain and more, in one or two columns
• Filters in three groups: colour looks, geometry (swirl, bulge, fisheye, mirror, waves, ripple, focus, zoom …) and art (kaleidoscope, tiny planet, glass tiles, tilt-shift); with strength, a centre and an area you set with your fingers; write your own filters as shaders
• Stack filters, try the next one on top
• Crop with fixed shapes, turn, straighten; zoom in to crop precisely
• A larger canvas (e.g. for a caption), frames inside or around the photo
• Draw, texts (seven fonts, outline, spacing, shadows) and stickers; move, resize and turn them with your fingers
• Templates: save a look and apply it to other photos with one tap
• Hold the photo to see it over the whole screen
• Information about the photo: folder, file name, size, camera, date

<b>Collages</b>
• Two side by side, two stacked, 2 × 2 or 3 × 3; move the lines, make the middle larger or smaller
• Choose several photos at once, swap them by dragging

<b>Videos</b>
• Keep a part, change the speed, mute, crop

<b>Made for your phone</b>
• Portrait and landscape: in landscape the photo on the left, the tools beside it
• Settings for the photo size in landscape, the rows and columns of filter thumbnails and more
• Save as PNG or JPG, into a folder of your choice
• Dark and light theme

100 % free and open source, no proprietary dependencies, no ads, no tracking.
